The short version

Problem

A Detroit startup building the “Life Utility Vehicle” lived across disconnected mini-sites with different branding, type and navigation.

What I did

Designed and built one unified site from scratch in Webflow, backed by a documented component library.

Outcome

chipmotors.com is live: 6+ pages built from 15 master components, used 82 times across the site.

From scattered to shipped
