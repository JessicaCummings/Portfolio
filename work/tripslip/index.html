TripSlip: field-trip chaos, turned into a 10-minute workflow

Designed by me, from the first interview to 100+ screens and a coded UI kit. Human-led, AI-accelerated.

My role
Product designer — research, UX, UI & design system
Research
7 interviews across 4 user groups
Timeline
June 2025 — ongoing
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Claude Design, Claude Code, Storybook
Mentorship
Newlab, Detroit

The short version

Problem

A field trip needs five parties — teachers, school admins, venues, bus companies and parents — and they coordinate through paper, email and spreadsheets. Teachers burn 10+ hours a week chasing it.

What I did

Ran 7 interviews, synthesized them in an affinity map, mapped the five-persona flow, and designed 3 of the 5 views — school admin, teacher and parent — across 100+ desktop, phone and tablet screens.

Outcome

Version 1 launched in September 2026. A trip goes from idea to ready in about 10 minutes, on a WCAG 2.2 AA UI kit every new screen starts from.

04 · Insight → decision

What the map told me to build

  • Collecting payments and slips is teachers' top priority.

    So I put slip and payment status at the top of the teacher's home screen.

  • Teachers want reusable trip types.

    So I made trip creation template-first, so a repeat trip takes minutes, not hours.

  • Teachers don't want more features — they want fewer decisions.

    So I mapped every tab first and cut overlapping features before any screen was drawn.

05 · Key design decisions

What I designed, and why

D.03 · Template-first trip creation

Start from a venue on TripSlip or add your own place. Either way it's five short steps — when, where, who, slip, review — and repeat trips start pre-filled.

06 · Design system, enhanced with AI

Human-led, AI-accelerated

I designed TripSlip. AI helped me get from decisions to a working, coded product faster — it never replaced talking to teachers.

What stayed human

  • All 7 interviews and the affinity map
  • The five-persona flow and every site map
  • Every product decision and trade-off
  • Brand direction, characters and accessibility rules

Where AI helped

  • Built 100+ screens and a clickable prototype in Claude Design
  • Extracted tokens from the Figma brand file into a React + TypeScript component library with Claude Code
  • Prepared a developer handoff for building with Claude Code
