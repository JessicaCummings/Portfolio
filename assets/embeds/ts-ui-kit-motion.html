Characters

Brand moments only, never over data.

Character waveOnly the arm moves, pivoting at the shoulder, while the body bobs 5px. Plays twice on arrival, then rests. Use it for welcome and success moments, one character per screen.

Dashed arrow

From trytripslip.com. Each dash draws in order (about 1.5s), then fills. It points from one section to the next thing to look at. Hover the card to replay.

  • Use it to point to the next thing to look at, like the Day-of pass or the signed-slip card on sign-in.
  • Plays once when it scrolls into view (under 5s, WCAG 2.2.2). Shows fully drawn with Reduce Motion or while editing.
  • Decorative only (aria-hidden). The words next to it carry the meaning.
