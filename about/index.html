01 · My story

Why I design

I know what it feels like when systems aren't built with you in mind.

I grew up in a rural town of 600 people, where information was harder to reach and opportunity wasn't evenly shared. That gap — between people who have access and people who don't — is what brought me to design.

At the University of Michigan I found civic tech and accessibility, and learned that everyday interfaces either include or exclude — usually depending on whether a designer asked the right questions. As the first in my family to graduate college, I take that personally.

Now I'm in Detroit designing fleet dashboards for Bridgestone, field-trip tools for K-12 schools and brand systems for automotive startups. Beautiful and functional — I don't think you should have to choose.
